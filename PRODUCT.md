# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Egyptian retail investors, aspiring investors, and financial professionals who want to understand EGX (Egyptian Exchange) stocks, mutual funds, and market dynamics. They use Starta Markets on desktop and mobile, often while making real investment decisions. They read Arabic natively but are comfortable switching to English for financial terminology. They range from first-time investors learning the basics to experienced traders seeking institutional-quality data.

## Product Purpose

Starta Markets is a premium financial intelligence platform for the Egyptian market. It surfaces real-time EGX data, mutual fund NAV and performance, market news, and an educational academy — all in a bilingual (Arabic/English) interface with dark and light themes. The platform positions itself between a retail brokerage and a Bloomberg terminal: accessible but serious, consumer-friendly but institutional in rigor.

## Brand Personality

Precision, confidence, clarity. The brand is the "trusted financial co-pilot": not flashy like a crypto exchange, not dry like a Bloomberg terminal. Serious enough for professionals, approachable enough for beginners. The visual language is premium near-black/midnight surfaces with teal accents in dark mode, warm white surfaces with teal accents in light mode.

## Anti-references

- Generic fintech SaaS cream landing pages (Robinhood-style gradients, bubbly illustrations)
- Crypto exchange neon-on-black maximalism
- Old-school Bloomberg terminal aesthetics (dense tabular data, no visual hierarchy)
- Rounded icon-above-heading card grids (template-looking, low-signal)
- em-dashes anywhere in copy

## Design Principles

1. **Precision over decoration.** Every visual element earns its place by communicating something — data, structure, hierarchy, or navigation. Decoration that doesn't inform is noise.
2. **Trust is earned through consistency.** The brand must look the same whether the user is in Arabic RTL mode, light theme, or on a small screen. Visual drift breaks trust.
3. **Premium means intentional density.** Not sparse minimalism, not cluttered maximalism — but deliberate information hierarchy where important data has room to breathe and secondary data is clearly secondary.
4. **Bilingual is a first-class feature.** The switch between English and Arabic must feel seamless, not bolted on. RTL layout should feel as designed as LTR.
5. **Motion amplifies meaning.** Animations exist to guide attention, signal state changes, and reward scroll progress — never to fill silence or show off.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Bilingual Arabic/English required throughout. RTL must be fully supported for Arabic (not just text flip — full layout mirror). `prefers-reduced-motion` must disable entrance animations. Color contrast ratios must hold in both dark and light themes.
